# ARC-2B — Amphibious transformation engineering study

Third study in the series (`docs/feasibility/` → `docs/architecture/` → here).
Question: can a genuinely engineered, mechanically integrated LAND → WATER →
LAND transformation make the 487 kg V0 vehicle amphibious without destroying
its land role?

**Answer: yes, at displacement speed (6 km/h cruise, 9 km/h maximum, sheltered
water), with Architecture H:** a watertight lower-body tub, fold-down fender
rails, two inflatable three-chamber tubes stored inside the fender cavities,
foam-filled cavities, and a stern vectoring pod; the wheels stay on their
carriers at the existing −2° stop. Planing and semi-planing are excluded on
the integrated vehicle and remain the separate ARC-2B M platform.

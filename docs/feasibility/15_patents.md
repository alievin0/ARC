## 15.3 Areas that need a patent attorney / FTO review before fabrication

1. **Track cassette design** (Camso, Soucy, Polaris/Timbersled continuations): highest commercial risk; consider licensing or purchase rather than in-house cassettes.
2. **Marine variant arch flaps and wheel-to-jet steering coupling** (WaterCar US 8,221,174 family; Gibbs 2011+ filings including US 11,766,908 B2).
3. **Actuated corner geometry on a rider-carrying vehicle** (Hyundai / Mobis / REE / CN wheel-leg families filed 2018–2024, none readable here).
4. **Electrically actuated reverse/trim** (BRP iBR-era, Teleflex).
5. **Multi-mode transformation interlocks** (Terrafugia breadth).

## 15.4 Potentially protectable ideas in this study (for the attorney's list, not a claim of novelty)

- Coaxial carrier/arm corner module with a spring reacting between arm and carrier, a self-locking positioning actuator outside the driving load path, and a spring-applied sector pin, combined with steering tie-rod inner joints on the pivot axis.
- Use of the corner actuation to unload one corner for a tool-free, jack-free swap of hub-mounted ski/track kits, with kit identification and drive-coupling verification performed with the corner lifted.
- Interlock architecture in which mode declaration requires hardware switch states and a non-programmable speed comparator gates the lock solenoids.
